## Worlds with people behind them

An early film concept built around Minecraft worlds as places shaped by their players. The visual direction explores how separate worlds might connect within a larger reality.

## Finding the visual language

The aim is to stay recognisably faithful to Minecraft while exploring cinematic composition, light and scale. This is concept development, with the story and production approach still open.
