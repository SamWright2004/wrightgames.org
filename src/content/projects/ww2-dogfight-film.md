## The project

A cinematic short film set in World War 2, about British Pilot engaging in a dogfight with an enemy fighter. The animation is being done entirely in Blender.

This project aims to convey emotional weight to characters not physically visible. As well as a proof of concept for my ability to construct a complex, fast paced action sequence.

## In development

The project is being developed through story work and planning for digital production. Finished footage is not yet presented here.
